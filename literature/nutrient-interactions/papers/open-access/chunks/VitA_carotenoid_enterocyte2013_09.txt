Чанк 9 из 14: 5. Conclusions
============================================================

To conclude, the full understanding of vitamin A and carotenoid absorption by the enterocyte is still in progress. Although some specific proteins such as the cytosolic CRBPII, and several non-specific transporters such as SR-BI, NPC1-L1, and ABCA1 have been identified, other transporters such as the dietary vitamin A apical membrane transporter remain to be identified.
