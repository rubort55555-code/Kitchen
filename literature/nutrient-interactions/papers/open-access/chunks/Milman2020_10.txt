Чанк 10 из 19: 4. Inhibitors (часть 4)
============================================================

Regular ingestion of curcumin is recommended by many nutritionists being in favor of anti-inflammatory diets. However, from an iron absorption point of view, curcumin may in the long term have an indirect enhancing effect on iron uptake because curcumin inhibits the synthesis of hepcidin through inhibition of the signal transducer and activator of transcription 3 (STAT3) transduction pathway. This effect has been demonstrated both in mice [139] and in humans [140]. Although curcumin may be of benefit for patients with iron overload due to its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, more studies are needed to clarify the final role of curcumin in human iron metabolism.
