Чанк 15 из 20: Validate the Predictive Equation
============================================================

Step 4, the concluding stage of the framework, focuses on validating the predictive equation when feasible and enhancing its applicability for practical implementation. The typical approach to validating an equation is to see how well data fit the equation from datasets not used to develop the equation (independent dataset) but that use experimental designs and endpoints consistent with the datasets that were used to develop the equation [107]. Preserving an independent dataset from equation development will be difficult to achieve for many nutrients and bioactive with existing literature.
