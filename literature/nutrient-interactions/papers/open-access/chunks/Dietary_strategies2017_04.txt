Чанк 4 из 15: IRON FORTIFICATION OF FOOD (часть 2)
============================================================

The Codex Alimentarius41 gives several options for determining the measure (Table 2). In general, these measures should be roughly equivalent. However, in the case of products that are consumed in small quantities but are nevertheless good carriers of fortification, such as condiments and bouillon cubes, the measures “100 g,” or “100 mL” or “100 kcal” are not realistic, since such quantities are not consumed in practice. Some companies estimate that the measure “per serving” is more appropriate to convey good nutrition,42 provided the serving is determined realistically and corresponds with the amounts typically consumed.
